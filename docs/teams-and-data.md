# Teams and data

## Teams

A team is a list of names with optional per-person settings. Create one with
**New → New Team**, edit it with **Edit → Edit Team**, and switch teams from
Settings (the panel slides in from the right). On the start screen, the timer
and chances stickers under the team name also open the team editor. Up to 25 teams of up to 100 people each.

Per person, in the team editor:

- **Chances** (1 to 5): tickets in the draw. See [Raffle](raffle.md#chances).
- **Timer**: overrides the mode's timer for this person only.

The app opens on a setup card. With the demo crew (15 of history's legends,
30-second timer) it rotates their one-liners in a random order. **Use my own
team →** lets you pick a saved team or paste names (one per line or
comma-separated); **Continue →** goes to the Start screen.

## Out today

Before you press **Start**, tap a name in the cloud to mark that person out
today. They sit out every mode for the rest of the day. Tap again to bring
them back.

## Share a team link

Settings → **Copy team link** copies a URL with the team encoded after `#team=`.
Opening it offers to add the team to that browser. The link carries names,
chances, and per-person timers, and it travels only inside the link itself. The
address bar tidies itself once the team is read.

## Export and import

Settings → **Export** downloads everything (teams, topic lists, question lists)
as one JSON file. **Import** reads such a file back: it checks the file first,
fills in defaults for any settings an older backup lacks, and replaces your saved
teams and lists once you confirm. Export a backup before importing. Import and
editing are available between sessions.

## Where data lives

Everything is saved in this browser, on your device:

- Each browser, device, and private window keeps its own data.
- Clearing site data resets the app, so export a backup of teams you care about.
- To move a team to another device, share a team link or export and import.
