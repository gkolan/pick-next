# Hot Seat

## What it does

Hot Seat picks one person and puts them in the seat for a round of rapid-fire,
timed questions.

## Run one

1. Choose **Hot Seat**.
2. Press **Start** or <kbd>Space</kbd>. The roulette picks the person.
3. The question card shows the question, `Q1 of 5`, and a countdown. Press
   **Answered** or **Skip** to move on.

When the countdown reaches zero, the question counts as skipped and the next one
starts automatically.

When the last question is done, the person has survived the hot seat and the
next pick is ready. **Progress** on the left lists everyone who has taken the
seat; their card turns green when their round is over.

**Exit Hot Seat** ends the session; the recap lists every completed round.
Press <kbd>Esc</kbd> during the pick animation to cancel that pick.

## Settings

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Question List | Demo questions |
| Questions per person | 5 |
| Seconds per question | 15 |

Choose these before starting; they stay fixed for the session.

## Question lists

In Hot Seat, **Edit → Edit Questions** changes the current list and
**New → New Questions** creates another. Up to 25 lists of up to 50 questions each.
